 4. NSE & Authentication Credential Attacks

Use authentication testing only after identifying the service and its authentication mechanism.

Recommended workflow:

  • Confirm the service is real and reachable.
  • Identify the authentication mechanism.
  • Check for default or weak credentials.
  • Perform normal enumeration first.
