Quick operator reference
  • site: limits results to a domain or subdomain.
  • "exact phrase" searches for words in that order.
  • OR matches either of multiple alternatives.
  • -word excludes a term; -inurl: excludes URL paths.
  • intitle: searches for a term in the page title.
  • inurl: searches for a term in the page URL.
  • filetype: filters by format, such as pdf or pptx.
  • after: and before: narrow dates, but coverage may be approximate.

Start with a simple query, add one operator at a time, and check that each result really belongs to the intended domain and date range. Syntax and coverage can vary; verify findings against approved sources and record when you searched.

RustScan

Fast TCP port discovery with Nmap service detection for the ports found. RustScan does not replace UDP enumeration or careful service analysis. Scan only systems included in your authorized scope.

Quick TCP Scan

Check the first 1,000 TCP ports, then run Nmap scripts and version detection on open ports.

 Full TCP & UDP Follow-up

Use the full TCP range when the quick scan is insufficient; UDP is a separate Nmap scan.

Masscan

High-speed TCP port discovery. Masscan does not identify services, so follow its results with Nmap. Keep the rate low for a single lab host; use subnet ranges only when explicitly authorized.

Discover Ports & Enumerate Services

Save open ports in list format, extract the TCP port numbers and pass them to Nmap.
